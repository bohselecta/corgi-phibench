already emphasizes minimal agents, configurable model/environment adapters,
linear trajectories and sandbox support, including Bubblewrap. PhiBench claims
no novelty for those ideas. Its contribution here is a small shared six-policy
laboratory, locked local manifests, explicit unknown/failure states and offline
receipt-derived inspection. No competitor performance claim is adopted.
